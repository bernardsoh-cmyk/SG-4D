About

Not affiliated with Singapore Pools

This app is an independent results viewer. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or connected to Singapore Pools or the Singapore Totalisator Board. You cannot place a bet here, and this app does not handle money.

Where the results come from

Results are read from Singapore Pools' published 4D draw pages. Official results are whatever Singapore Pools publishes — if this app and their site ever disagree, their site is right.

About the prize figures

4D prizes are fixed amounts per $1 stake, taken from Singapore Pools' published 4D Game Rules, so the checker works them out exactly rather than estimating.

iBet is not the fixed prize divided by the number of permutations — it has its own rate for each digit pattern, and the checker uses those published rates.

If gambling stops being fun

Set your own limits and stick to them. Help is available in Singapore from the National Problem Gambling Helpline on 1800-6-668-668.
