About

Not affiliated with Singapore Pools

This app is an independent results viewer for 4D, TOTO and Singapore Sweep. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or connected to Singapore Pools or the Singapore Totalisator Board. You cannot place a bet here, and this app does not handle money.

Where the results come from

Results are read from Singapore Pools' published draw pages. Official results are whatever Singapore Pools publishes — if this app and their site ever disagree, their site is right.

About the prize figures

4D pays fixed amounts per $1 stake, taken from Singapore Pools' published 4D Game Rules, so the checker works them out exactly. iBet is not the fixed prize divided by the permutations — it has its own rate for each digit pattern, and the checker uses those published rates.

TOTO Groups 5, 6 and 7 are fixed at $50, $25 and $10. Groups 1 to 4 are a share of that draw's prize pool, so the amount only exists once the draw is over. Where the published share is known the checker uses it; where it is not, it says so rather than guessing.

Singapore Sweep group prizes are fixed and printed in each draw's own results, so the checker reports the figure from the draw itself. The top three prizes are not published alongside the numbers, so a win there is reported without an amount rather than with a wrong one.
